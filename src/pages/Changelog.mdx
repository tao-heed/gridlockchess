### Added

- **Sandbox mode.** A free-form position editor — place any Kings, Pawns, and
  Anomalies, choose each Anomaly's charge build (or make one a **Piloted Anomaly**),
  set the side to move, then play it out Pass &amp; Play or against a bot. Opened from the
  Opponent menu.
- **Save & replay games to the Sandbox.** From a game's Replay panel, save the whole
  game — or a rewound point — into your Sandbox library, then re-open it to scrub
  through every move.

### Changed

- **Balanced armies are now exactly macro-balanced.** Every army's charges sum
  to exactly 24 / 23 / 23 across Leap / Orthogonal / Diagonal, drawn by a uniform
  sampler (no re-rolls, no lopsided hands). This is now the only generation mode.
  The reachable opening pool is **5,950,517,760** distinct positions (down from
  the earlier ~372 billion, which allowed lopsided armies).

### Fixed

### Removed

- **Balanced / Exact mode toggle.** The two-mode split is gone; every game uses
  the single Balanced (24 / 23 / 23) generator. Old saved games that recorded a
  `pure` or `exact` mode load as Balanced.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-06-28

The first public release. Everything below is new.

### Gameplay

- **Random Anomaly army.** Each game, your back row is rolled fresh — seven
  Anomalies with randomized stats, mirrored for both sides, so there is zero
  material luck. Over 372 billion unique starting positions.
- **Charge system.** Every piece moves along three vectors (Leap, Orthogonal,
  Diagonal), and each move burns one charge from the vector it used. Charges
  never come back. Spend a vector dry and that direction is gone for good.
- **Gridlock.** A piece with all three vectors exhausted (0/0/0) Gridlocks —
  it is stranded on the board, unable to move.
- **Override.** A desperate, one-way gamble: step your King onto a friendly
  Anomaly to fuse into a single royal piece. It moves like its host, burns a
  charge per move, can still be checked, and loses instantly if its charges run
  dry.
- **AI opponent** powered by the Fairy-Stockfish engine, with multiple
  difficulty tiers.
- **Online play.** Challenge a friend over a private passcode-keyed room.

### Interface

- Animated board with drag-and-drop and click-to-move, each legal move lit in
  its vector's color.
- Quick Start walkthrough for first-time players.
- In-app Rules reference and Licenses & Credits pages.
- Sound effects and a mute toggle.
