Gridlock Chess is a chess variant built around one fascinating
question: *what if every piece could run out of moves?* It keeps the board, the King,
and the pawns you already know—and rebuilds everything else around finite, spendable
power.

This page is the *why* behind the project. If you just want to play, the
Quick Start button gets you moving in a minute. If you want the exact ruleset, the
[Rules](/rules) cover every mechanic in detail.

## Why It Exists

Standard chess tends to wear players down in two ways: openings reward rote
memorization over creative thinking, and a single early material advantage can quietly
choke the life out of a game long before the interesting part begins.

Gridlock Chess is an experiment in solving both—without throwing away the tactical
depth that makes chess great.

The bet is simple: if every piece carries a limited, depleting energy supply, then
resource management becomes a new layer of skill. You stop asking only *"what's the
best move?"* and start asking *"what can I afford to spend?"*

## What It Is

- **Free and frictionless:** No accounts, no installs, no paywalls. Open the page and
  play.
- **Protocol: Run Dry:** A structured solo progression — fight through a ladder of
  increasingly capable AI opponents to prove full mastery of the charge system.
- **Offline and Online:** Play against the AI, or pass the device with a friend in
  Pass &amp; Play — no connection needed. Take it online to challenge a friend with a
  private room code, or jump into a Quick Match against a random opponent.
- **Build any position:** A Sandbox editor lets you hand-craft a board — choose the
  pieces and their charges — then play it out Pass &amp; Play or against a bot.
- **Save and replay:** Every game can be saved to your Sandbox library so you can
  scrub through every move exactly as it was played.
- **An indie experiment:** This is not a massive studio production. It is a focused
  passion project, built and refined in the open, with the design kept deliberately
  approachable.

## The Philosophy

A few principles guide every design choice:

- **Finite power, real decisions.** Scarcity creates tension. When moves are a resource
  you can exhaust, every turn carries weight.
- **Variety without luck.** Randomized armies keep each game fresh, but both sides
  always receive an identical, mirrored force. The outcome is earned, never drawn from a
  lucky hand.
- **Easy to start, hard to master.** The familiar board lowers the entry barrier; the
  charge system raises the ceiling.
